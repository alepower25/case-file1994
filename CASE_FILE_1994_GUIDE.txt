------------------------------------------------------------------
  ABOUT THE GAME
------------------------------------------------------------------

  CASE FILE 1994 is a top-down 2D horror game built in HTML5.
  You play as Detective Harris, a private investigator who receives
  an anonymous tip about the Aldric Building, Block 7, where
  47 city workers vanished in 1987.

  The case was sealed by government order.
  Official report: gas leak.
  The truth: something else entirely.

  November 5, 1994. 3:06 AM. You are going in.

  There is more than one way this ends.
  Most players will not find all of them.


------------------------------------------------------------------
  COMPLETION  --  STAR SYSTEM
------------------------------------------------------------------

  Your completion state is tracked permanently across sessions
  and displayed on the title screen.

  NO STARS
    The case is open. You have not yet reached the end.

  STAR STAR  (★★)  --  red
    [ NO MERCY CLEARED ]
    You found the secret level and survived it.
    This is not easy. Most players will not reach this.

  STAR STAR STAR  (★★★)  --  gold
    [ 100% COMPLETE ]
    You have cleared the main game on INSANE difficulty.
    The title screen will never look the same again.
    There is nothing left to find.

    ...

    Or is there?


------------------------------------------------------------------
  CONTROLS
------------------------------------------------------------------

  MOVE ................. W / A / S / D  or  Arrow Keys
  SPRINT ............... Hold SHIFT  (drains Stamina)
  SNEAK ................ Hold C  (slower movement, very low noise)
  FLASHLIGHT ........... F  (drains Battery, restores Sanity)
  RECHARGE BATTERY ..... R  (charges from reserve; limited uses)
  FIELD NOTES .......... N  (view collected lore documents)
  BRIEFING TAB ......... N then TAB  (re-read Harris's audio logs)
  ADVANCE / INTERACT ... SPACE  or  Left Click
  PAUSE ................ P  or  ESC
  SETTINGS ............. S  (from title screen only)
  PATCH NOTES .......... P  (from title screen)
  INSTRUCTIONS ......... H  (from title screen, re-accessible)

  NOTE: A HELP SCREEN showing all controls appears the first
  time you begin the game. Press SPACE, ESC, or click to dismiss.
  It can be accessed again at any time from the title screen  [ H ].


  OBJECTIVE:
    Explore each level and collect all evidence tapes (or the key).
    Each tape gives you a letter clue  --  for example,  B = R
    Once all evidence is collected, reach the EXIT.
    At the exit, decode the encoded word using your clues.
    Type the correct word to unlock the next level.

  NOTE ON CIPHER WORDS:
    The encoded word and clues are RANDOMIZED each run.
    The word belongs to the same category per level,
    but the specific encoding changes every time you play.
    Memorizing answers from a previous run will not help.


------------------------------------------------------------------
  HARRIS STATUS (HUD)
------------------------------------------------------------------

  HEALTH
    Depletes when a monster catches you.
    Reaching zero is game over.

  SANITY
    Drains when the flashlight is OFF or a monster is nearby.
    Low sanity causes visual distortion and hallucinations.
    Recovers when the flashlight is ON with battery remaining.
    In the secret level, certain light sources restore Sanity.

  BATTERY
    Drains while the flashlight is active.
    When battery hits zero, the flashlight dies.
    Sanity then drains rapidly.

  STAMINA
    Drains when sprinting.
    Walking slowly recovers Stamina.
    Standing still recovers Stamina faster.

  NOISE  (Levels 4 and 5 only)
    Tracks how loud Harris is being.
    Rises when walking or sprinting, falls when standing still.
    Picking up tapes or documents causes a noise spike.
    The Listener uses this to find you.
    Green = safe. Orange = caution. Red = danger.


------------------------------------------------------------------
  MONSTERS
------------------------------------------------------------------

  There are three distinct entities in Case File 1994.
  Each one behaves differently. Each one requires
  a completely different survival strategy.


  THE FIGURE  (Levels 1, 2, 3)
  ................................................................

  A tall, dark silhouette. Hunts by SIGHT.
  It watches. It waits. Then it moves.

  BEHAVIORAL STATES:

    LURKING
      Passive. Wanders and observes from a distance.
      Keeps a minimum gap from the player.
      The longer it lurks, the sooner it turns aggressive.

    ABSENT
      Teleports out of sight entirely.
      Can reappear anywhere in the level without warning.

    AGGRESSIVE
      Pursues the player directly.
      Triggered by prolonged proximity or exposure.
      At Level 3, it can teleport to the player's position
      if given the opportunity (3+ encroachments, low Sanity).

  HOW TO SURVIVE THE FIGURE:

    RED SCREEN EDGES
      The Figure is within 5 tiles. Move away immediately.

    STARE-BACK
      If the Figure is visible and you face it,
      it freezes briefly. Use this to create distance.
      NOTE: On INSANE difficulty, staring only works twice.
      After that, the Figure encroaches regardless.

    SAFE ROOMS
      Each level has one small room (green-tinted floor)
      where the Figure will not enter. Use it to recover.

    FLASHLIGHT
      Keep it ON. Low Sanity makes the Figure more dangerous
      by impairing your ability to navigate and think.


  THE LISTENER  (Levels 4, 5)
  ................................................................

  A hunched creature with large ear spikes. Hunts by SOUND.
  It is completely blind. It cannot see you at all.
  But it hears everything.

  BEHAVIORAL STATES:

    PATROL
      Wanders slowly and randomly.
      Not aware of your presence.
      A faint purple glow marks its opacity.

    ALERTED
      Heard you. Moving fast toward your last noise position.
      A purple ring pulses around it.
      It will not stop until it reaches that position.
      If you are quiet before it arrives -- it may calm down.

  HOW TO SURVIVE THE LISTENER:

    STAND STILL
      Standing still rapidly drops your Noise level.
      The Listener cannot find you if there is no sound.

    WALK, DO NOT SPRINT
      Walking produces low noise. Sprinting produces very
      loud noise and will alert the Listener from far away.

    TAPE PICKUPS MAKE NOISE
      Every tape or document you collect causes a noise spike.
      After picking one up -- stand still immediately.

    PURPLE SCREEN EDGES
      The Listener is alerted and very close.
      Stop moving. Let your noise level drop.

    SAFE ROOMS
      The Listener also will not enter the safe room.
      Use it to fully calm down before continuing.

    NO STARE-BACK
      The Listener cannot be frozen by looking at it.
      Silence is your only defense.


  [ENTITY DESIGNATION: CLASSIFIED]  (Secret Level only)
  ................................................................

  Something else is in the building.
  It was not in any of the reports.

  It does not lurk. It does not patrol.
  It chases.

  You cannot fight it. You cannot freeze it.
  The only option is to hide.
  Choose carefully. It hears lockers.

  Further information has been sealed by Dept. order 0312.
  If you encounter it -- you already know what it looks like.


------------------------------------------------------------------
  DIFFICULTY SETTINGS
------------------------------------------------------------------

  Select from the Settings screen (press S on the title).

  VERY EASY  (Crybaby Mode)
    Larger health, sanity, and stamina pools.
    +10% movement speed bonus.
    Figure reduced speed. Extended cipher timer.
    A gift battery is provided on Level 0.
    Certain document text is simplified.

  EASY
    Figure speed reduced 30%.
    Figure lurks much longer before going aggressive.
    Cipher timer doubled to 6 minutes.
    Listener detects noise only above 48%.
    Listener is slower and calms down quickly.

  NORMAL  (recommended)
    All stats tuned approximately 5% in favor of the player.
    Figure and Listener both slightly slower than base.
    Cipher timer: 3 minutes.

  HARD
    Figure speed increased 65%. Lurk time cut by 55%.
    Cipher timer halved to 90 seconds.
    Listener detects noise at 12% (almost any movement).
    Listener speed increased 65%. Hearing range 55% wider.
    Listener needs noise below 6% before it stops chasing.

  INSANE
    Everything is faster. The Figure no longer tolerates
    being stared at indefinitely. The Listener senses at
    proximity regardless of noise level.
    Cipher timer: 45 seconds.
    Completing INSANE mode unlocks something.
    You will know when it happens.

  SECRET CODE
    There is a field in the Settings screen.
    Type something and press ENTER.
    Pay attention to the documents you collect.
    Certain letters are not where they should be.

------------------------------------------------------------------
  ENVIRONMENTAL HAZARDS  --  MECHANICS
------------------------------------------------------------------

  CREAK TILES
  ................................................................

  Certain floor tiles throughout the building are structurally
  compromised. When stepped on, they produce a loud creak.

  Visual  : Slightly darker floor tint with a faint X pattern.

  Effect  :
    Levels 1-3  : The Figure immediately enters its aggressive
                  state. Create distance as fast as possible.
    Levels 4-5  : Your NOISE level spikes by +40 immediately.
                  Stop moving at once and let it drop.

  There is a short cooldown after each creak (roughly 3 seconds)
  so the same tile will not keep triggering repeatedly.


  LOCKED SIDE ROOMS  --  KEYCARD
  ................................................................

  Each level contains a keycard item hidden on the floor.
  Somewhere in the level there is also a locked side room.

  KEYCARD LOCATIONS:
    Level 1  :  Tile (12, 1)  --  upper archive section
    Level 2  :  Tile (6,  4)  --  near east stairwell
    Level 3  :  Tile (10, 1)  --  top corridor, north hall
    Level 4  :  Tile (3,  2)  --  near player spawn, northwest
    Level 5  :  Tile (3,  2)  --  northwest corner

  The locked room contains a classified lore document.
  Walk over the document to read it and add it to Field Notes.

  NOTE: On Levels 4-5, picking up the document causes a noise
  spike. Stand still immediately after collecting it.

  NOTE ON DOCUMENT TEXT:
    Read the documents carefully. On Normal difficulty and above,
    certain words contain letters that should not be capitalized.
    These are not formatting errors.


  LOCKER ROOMS  (Secret Level only)
  ................................................................

  Lockers are scattered across the secret level.
  Press SPACE near one to enter.

  If the entity is far away -- it will search your last known
  position, then wander. Exit when it moves away.

  If the entity heard you enter -- it will come directly to the
  locker and break it open. You will not survive this.

  Distance from the locker determines whether it is safe to exit.
  The game will tell you if it is too close.


  LIGHT SANCTUARIES  (Secret Level only)
  ................................................................

  Scattered across the secret level are small glowing spots.
  Walking through one restores 20% Sanity.
  Each can only be used once per run.

  Prioritize these when Sanity drops below 30.
  Below 30 Sanity, hallucinations begin.
  What you see in the dark may not be there.


  SCRIPTED EVENTS  --  ONE-TIME ATMOSPHERIC TRIGGERS
  ................................................................

  At specific locations on each level, one-time events will
  trigger automatically when Harris enters the area.

    DOOR SLAM
      A door slams somewhere deep in the building.
      Causes brief camera shake.

    LIGHT FLICKER
      The flashlight cuts out for 0.8 to 1.4 seconds.
      Your Sanity will drop during the blackout.

    SHAPE FLASH
      A dark shape appears briefly at the edge of the beam.
      Accompanied by a burst of static.
      Nothing is there when you reach the position.
      (Figure levels only.)

  All scripted events fire only once per level load.


------------------------------------------------------------------
  TIPS AND STRATEGY
------------------------------------------------------------------

  GENERAL:

  - Keep the flashlight ON. Low Sanity causes distortion
    that makes navigation and cipher entry much harder.

  - Memorize your clues BEFORE walking to the exit.
    Neither monster pauses while you type the answer.
    Panic is the real enemy.

  - Learn your current level's monster type.
    Different monsters require completely different strategies.

  - Each level has a SAFE ROOM (faint green floor tint).
    Both monsters avoid it entirely. Use it to recover.

  - Field Notes (press N) stores every lore document you find.
    Read them. They contain more than lore.


  VS THE FIGURE (Levels 1-3):

  - Sprint only in emergencies. Stare back to freeze it briefly.
  - On INSANE, staring only works twice. Then it comes anyway.
  - If screen edges glow RED, the Figure is within 5 tiles.
    Create distance immediately.
  - At Level 3, stay above 45 Sanity to prevent teleportation.
  - THE FIGURE LEARNS: If you visit the same corridor 3+ times,
    that area becomes a hot zone. Vary your routes.
  - Avoid CREAK TILES -- they push the Figure into aggression.


  VS THE LISTENER (Levels 4-5):

  - NEVER sprint. It hears sprinting from across the entire map.
  - After every tape or document pickup, STAND STILL immediately.
  - If the screen edges glow PURPLE, stop all movement and wait.
  - The Listener moves toward where it HEARD you last --
    not where you are now. Use this to mislead it.
  - Standing completely still will eventually cause it to
    lose interest and return to patrol.

  SNEAK MODE  (hold C while moving):

  - Moves at 62% of walk speed.
  - Generates almost no noise: 0.28 per frame vs 1.2 for walking.
  - USE IT when the Listener is nearby and you must still move.

  INVOLUNTARY BREATH:
    While sneaking, Harris cannot hold his breath forever.
    Every 3 to 6 seconds, an involuntary exhale spikes noise.
    On HARD and INSANE, every breath can get you caught.
    When the [C] SNEAKING label turns orange -- a breath is
    imminent. Consider stopping and letting it pass.


  VS THE SECRET ENTITY:

  - It does not have states. It chases. That is all it does.
  - The only defense is a locker. Learn where they are first.
  - If it is far away when you hide, it will search your last
    position and wander. Wait for it to move away.
  - If it heard you enter the locker, it will break in.
    Exit the locker BEFORE hiding in that situation.
  - It speeds up each time you find a body part.
    Plan your route. Efficient is safer than fast.
  - The fog of war is heavy. Keep your flashlight on.


------------------------------------------------------------------
  VISUAL NOTES
------------------------------------------------------------------

  All footage is rendered as a degraded VHS recording from 1994.

  FILM GRAIN
    Persistent analog noise across all footage.

  CHROMATIC ABERRATION
    Color fringing at screen edges (red left, cyan right).

  SHADOW CASTING
    The flashlight does not pass through walls.
    Monsters outside your beam cannot be seen.
    This works in your favor -- and against it.

  FOG OF WAR  (Secret Level only)
    The secret level is darker than anything in Levels 1-5.
    A small ambient radius is always visible around Harris.
    Beyond it: nothing. Keep the flashlight on.

  100% COMPLETE TITLE SCREEN
    If you have fully completed the game, the title screen
    changes permanently. The background is different.
    The music is different.
    This is intentional.

  VHS GLITCH EFFECTS
    Tracking error bands drift across the screen.
    Horizontal desync flashes simulate tape damage.
    These are purely aesthetic. The footage is from 1994.
